Renders the meta line under a post entry - typically the publish date and author byline. Included from template-parts/content/entry.php for single posts and archive listings.

Usage

get_template_part( 'template-parts/content/entry-meta' );

Notes

  • Only renders for the post post type.
  • Pulls formatting from wp_rig()->posted_on() and wp_rig()->posted_by().
