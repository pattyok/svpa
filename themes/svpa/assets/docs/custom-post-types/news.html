Template

The posts are displayed with a custom header that includes the image, title, byline and date.

The Featured Image for the news post should be a landscape oriented photo.

Byline
The byline is displayed under the title in the layout.
The input for that is under the meta boxes at the bottom of the edit view.

You may need to drag the handle up to see the field.

Sort Order

News posts are displayed in order by publish date. You can modify the publish date in the page settings pane.
