## Template

The team lists are displayed on the Our Team page, filtered by team type.

Each person should get assigned
* Photo (staff) - displayed as square will be cropped when displayed
* Job Title
* Bio (content) - if displayed
* Additional (board) - used to add their workplace
* Menu Order
